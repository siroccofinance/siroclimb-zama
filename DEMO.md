# Siroclimb on Zama: 3-minute demo script

For the Zama Developer Program, Season 5 video, recorded by a person on screen with voice-over.
Have ready a Sepolia wallet holding about 0.05 ETH, a US market session if you want the stocks
open (or use gold and GBP), and a second tab on Sepolia Etherscan.

## 0:00 to 0:20 · The hook

*Screen: the landing page, the coin rolling on the terracotta stairs. Click "Start playing".*

> "This is Siroclimb by Sirocco. You pick a real world asset (Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, gold or the
> pound), go long or short, and the coin climbs the stairs with the price. Every 6 seconds your
> leverage steps up, from 1x to 4x. Cash out whenever you want. And on Zama, nobody can see which
> way you went or how much you put in."

## 0:20 to 0:50 · Connect once, play without pop-ups

*Click Connect wallet. Approve the two wallet transactions. The status line shows each step.*

> "Two wallet transactions, once. The first approves the game as operator of my confidential USDC,
> Zama's ERC-7984 token. The second starts a game session: a key in my browser that plays for me,
> so a run never waits on a pop-up. It can play with what I approved, nothing else."

*The faucet runs by itself. Click the balance, which shows "Decrypt", and sign the decryption grant.*

> "My balance is encrypted on chain. I decrypt it here, in my browser, with a signature that's
> valid for the day. Nobody else can read it, not even the game's deployer."
