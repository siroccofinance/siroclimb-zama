        How it works

        1. Pick a market, a direction and a stake. Tokenized stocks, gold or the pound, long or short.
        2. The coin rolls on the live price. When the price goes your way it climbs a step, when it turns it steps down. Big moves, big jumps. Your leverage grows every 6 seconds, from 1x to 4x.
        3. Cash out whenever you want. If the price runs too far against you, the stairs give way and the coin falls. You lose your stake, never more.

        Rounds

        60 seconds on the same market for everyone. The top 3 share the pot: 50%, 30% and 20%.

        Private by design

        Your direction and your stake are encrypted on chain with Zama's FHE. Nobody can copy or fade you, not even in a round. Your result is computed on ciphertexts and paid in the same transaction; round standings stay sealed until the bell.

        Sepolia test network, Zama's confidential test USDC. Real prices (Pyth and public market data); every move counts 25x, then your leverage. Stocks trade during US market hours.
