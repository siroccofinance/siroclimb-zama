      How it works

      1. Pick a confidential token, a direction and a stake. cTSLAx, cNVDAx, cAAPLx, cAMZNx (Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, Amazon), cXAUt (gold) or ctGBP (the pound), long or short.
      2. The coin rolls on the live price. When the price goes your way it climbs a step, when it turns it steps down. Big moves, big jumps. Your leverage grows every 6 seconds, from 1x to 4x.
      3. The leverage is borrowed. At 3x on a 50 cUSDC stake you borrow 100 cUSDC from the Sirocco Reserve, a lending pool, and pay interest on it for the seconds you hold it.
      4. Cash out whenever you want. If the price runs too far against you, the stairs give way and the coin falls. You lose your stake, never more.

      Private by design

      Your direction and your stake are encrypted on chain with Zama's FHE. Nobody can copy or fade you. Your result is computed on ciphertexts and paid in the same transaction.

      Sepolia test network, Zama's confidential test USDC. Real prices: the stocks follow their xStock (the token cTSLAx and the others wrap), which trades 24/7; every move counts 25x, then your leverage. ctGBP follows GBP/USD, closed on weekends.

      Sirocco Reserve

      The lending pool behind every run's leverage. Lenders earn the borrow interest and carry the players' gains and losses. Deposits and shares are encrypted: only you can read yours.

      Your positionConnect to see it

      Totals are revealed by Zama's KMS at most every 10 minutes and checked on chain. Deposits and withdrawals use the share price of the last reveal. A withdrawal is all or nothing: it waits if the cash is lent to live runs.
